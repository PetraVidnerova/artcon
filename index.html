ARTCON – Artificial Consciousness Literature

Interactive exploration of ~696 academic papers on artificial consciousness. Papers are represented as nodes in a semantic similarity graph using SPECTER2 embeddings (original and bibliographic-coupling fine-tuned). Switch between similarity and keyword edges, apply clustering, highlight thesis stances, and open individual paper pages for full details.
